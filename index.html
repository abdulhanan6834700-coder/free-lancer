About Ferro Studio

A small studio, built so every client gets a founder's attention.

We stay deliberately small. That means the person who scopes your project is the same person who reviews the final build — nothing gets lost between a sales call and delivery.

Why we exist

Most small businesses lose customers before a conversation even starts — because the website looks unfinished, loads slowly, or never actually asks the visitor to do anything.

Ferro Studio was started to fix that specific problem: sites that look credible and are built around a clear next step, whether that's a booking, an enquiry, or a sale.

How we work

Every project runs the same way, so you always know what's coming next and what we need from you.

01

Discovery call

A 20-minute call to understand what you sell, who buys it, and what the site needs to achieve.
Day 1
02

Structure & copy

We map every page and draft the words before any visual design starts, so the site is built around a clear message.
Day 2–3
03

Design & build

Visual design and development happen together, with one round of review before the final build.
Day 4–5
04

Launch & handover

We publish the site and walk you through updating text, images and prices yourself.
